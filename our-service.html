Personal Shopper & Logistics, Done Right

🛍️ Personal Shopper (Jasa Titip)

Shop in Sydney, Without Being There

WonderOz JasTip's Personal Shopper service lets you buy from Sydney stores without visiting in person. Just send us the product link you want — our team handles everything else, from purchase and quality check through to shipment to Indonesia.

Service Fee: AUD 25 (minimum 5 items)

Shipping cost to Jakarta is calculated separately based on actual weight and the latest air freight rate, and will be confirmed before shipment.

  • ✓ Store/seller communication — confirming stock availability directly with the store
  • ✓ Product purchase — matched to your requested color, size, and variant
  • ✓ Quality check — every item is inspected before it's shipped
  • ✓ Collection & consolidation — items are gathered and neatly packed before sending to Indonesia
🚚 Logistics & Cargo

Fair Pricing, No Surprise Fees

Our Logistics service ensures every parcel is calculated fairly and clearly, with no hidden costs. We apply international weight standards (actual vs. volumetric) and provide packing guidance so your goods arrive safely.

Maximum 20 kg per box (actual or volumetric weight). Heavier parcels may incur an additional fee or be split into multiple boxes.

  • ✓ Accurate weight calculation — comparing actual weight against volumetric weight
  • ✓ Rate transparency — the pricing basis is confirmed before your item ships
  • ✓ Safe packing guidance — recommended packing methods for your items
  • ✓ Direct consultation — our team can advise on weight, size, or packing
