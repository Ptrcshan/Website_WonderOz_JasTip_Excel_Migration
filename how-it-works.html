🚚 Logistics

For air freight, two weight calculations are used — whichever is higher gets billed.

1

Weigh Actual Weight

The item's true weight, measured on a scale.

2

Calculate Volumetric Weight

Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm) ÷ 6000 = Volumetric Weight (kg)

3

Take the Higher Figure

Compare actual weight against volumetric weight.

4

That's What Gets Billed

The higher weight becomes the Billed Weight on your invoice.

Example 1Example 2
Parcel Size (L×W×H)50 × 30 × 40 cm30 × 20 × 25 cm
Actual Weight2 kg5 kg
Volumetric Weight10 kg2.5 kg
Billed Weight10 kg (10 kg > 2 kg)5 kg (5 kg > 2.5 kg)

Packing Guidelines Before Shipping:

Use a sturdy double-layer boxNot soft or damaged — a strong box protects your items throughout the journey.
Line with bubble wrap or foamAdd protective material inside the box before placing your items.
Fill empty spacePack the box so items don't shift or move around during transit.
Seal tightly with strong tapeTape at least 3 sides so the box stays firmly closed.
Seal liquids in tightly closed bagsLiquids must be double-bagged to prevent leaks during shipping.
Label fragile items clearlyLayer fragile items carefully and mark the box "FRAGILE".
Write the full address clearlyInclude the recipient's full address and contact number on the outside.

Important to Know:

  • Always measure and weigh your parcel correctly before shipping
  • Make sure size is in centimeters (cm) and weight is in kilograms (kg)
  • Invoices use Billed Weight (the higher of actual and volumetric)
  • Recommended maximum of 20 kg per box
  • If in doubt, consult our team directly
